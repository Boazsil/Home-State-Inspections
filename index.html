What's Inspected

A complete picture of the home

Every inspection follows the same disciplined process, covering the systems that matter most before closing.

Structure & Roof

Foundation, framing, attic, and roof covering, flashing, and drainage.

Electrical

Panel, wiring type and condition, outlets, GFCI/AFCI protection, and fixtures.

HVAC

Furnace, A/C, ductwork, and thermostat operation and condition.

Plumbing

Supply lines, drains, water heater, fixtures, and visible leaks.

Interior & Exterior

Windows, doors, walls, ceilings, grading, siding, and drainage around the home.

Safety Items

Smoke/CO detectors, egress, railings, and other health & safety checks.

Included With Every Full Inspection

Six free warranty programs, at no extra cost

An inspection tells you the condition of the home on the day it was inspected. These go further — if something that was working then fails afterward, you're covered. All six come free with a full home inspection, through Inspector Services Group.

90-Day Warranty

$500 mechanical (plumbing, electrical, appliances, HVAC) and $2,000 structural, with a $0 deductible. Runs 90 days from inspection or 22 days after closing, whichever is later.

SewerGard

Up to $2,000 each for water supply and waste line repairs. A sewer scope with a clear result stretches this from 90 days to six months.

MoldSafe

Up to $2,000 toward remediation if new visible mold appears after an inspection that reported none.

Platinum Roof Protection

Up to $3,000 in roof leak repairs for five years after your inspection — and it transfers if you sell.

RecallChek

A recalled appliance sits in about one in nine homes. Your appliances and HVAC get checked against a recall database, and you're emailed for as long as you own the home.

Full Concierge

Help getting utilities switched on after you move — power, water, gas, trash, internet — plus negotiated rates on alarms, insurance, and movers.
