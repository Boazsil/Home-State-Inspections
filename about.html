About Boaz Silverberg

Owner & Inspector, Home State Inspections

Local & Licensed

Straight answers about the home you're about to buy

Boaz Silverberg founded Home State Inspections to give Cincinnati-area buyers, sellers, and agents something simple: a thorough, honest look at a home's condition, explained in plain language, from someone who's easy to reach if a question comes up later.

Before inspecting homes, Boaz spent his career in production and manufacturing leadership, with a background in industrial engineering. That background isn't part of the inspection itself — it's why he approaches every walkthrough the same disciplined way: a consistent process, careful documentation, and no shortcuts, so nothing gets missed and nothing gets overstated.

Boaz is an Ohio state-licensed home inspector (License #OHI.2026002101), meeting the Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board's requirements — training, the National Home Inspector Examination, and General Liability / Errors & Omissions insurance. He's based in Blue Ash and inspects homes throughout the Greater Cincinnati area.

He also holds an FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate — the federal certification required to operate a drone commercially.

What to expect on inspection day

  • On time, in a marked vehicle, ready to walk you through anything on site
  • A full top-to-bottom review — roof to foundation
  • A same-day digital report with photos, sent straight to your inbox
  • A follow-up call to walk through anything that needs explaining
