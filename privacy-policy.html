Privacy Policy

Home State Inspections ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information this website collects and how it's used.

Information we collect

When you submit the quote request form on this site, the information you provide — name, email, phone number, property address, and any message you include — is sent directly to Boaz Silverberg's email inbox via your own email application. This site does not store or process that information itself.

When you use the online scheduler linked from this site, your booking information is collected by that third-party scheduling provider (Inspection Support Network) under its own privacy terms.

How we use it

Information submitted through this site is used only to respond to your inquiry or booking, and is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to boaz@homestateinspects.com.
